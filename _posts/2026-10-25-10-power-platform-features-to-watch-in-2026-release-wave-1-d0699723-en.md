## TL;DR

Power Platform 2026 Release Wave 1 includes Copilot Studio agents optimized for Microsoft 365 Copilot, enterprise evaluation for declarative agents, globally available generative pages, deleted-flow recovery, unattended-run video logs and Dataverse record restoration for up to 30 days.

## Why it matters

- Makers: Test deleted-flow recovery in the Maker Portal and review the modern model-driven app look before its April 2026 enforcement.
- Makers: Explore globally available generative pages and Copilot Studio agents optimized for Microsoft 365 and Microsoft 365 Copilot users.
- Admins/IT: Track Git-based deployment pipelines, scheduled for public preview in April, while noting that no general availability date is announced.
