- question: How can Power Automate handle employee leave approvals?
  answer: Power Automate can detect a leave request, send an approval to the manager,

- question: When should I choose a canvas app or model-driven app for employee records?
  answer: Choose a canvas app when screen layout and task-specific user experience

    and standardised behaviour.

    enable specific actions.

## TL;DR

Power Apps can capture employee details and leave requests, Power Automate can route manager approvals and update records, and Power BI can report on the results. Dataverse can hold structured data, while Copilot Studio agents can answer employee questions when configured with suitable knowledge and tools.

## Why it matters

- Makers: Start with a defined scenario such as leave approvals, then map users, data, process steps and reporting needs before selecting components.
- BI/Data teams: Design dashboards around a decision, then choose measures and visuals that help users identify results and trends.
- Leadership/Business: Combine an employee app, approval flow and reporting to replace spreadsheet-driven processes with a connected workflow.
