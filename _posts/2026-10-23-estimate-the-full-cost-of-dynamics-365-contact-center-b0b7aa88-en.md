## TL;DR

The Dynamics CCaaS Cost Calculator estimates Dynamics 365 Contact Center licences, telephony, Copilot Studio credits, AI agent consumption, Dataverse capacity, implementation and support. It accepts editable USD, EUR or GBP defaults, models month-by-month and yearly costs, and exports estimates with their assumptions to Excel and PDF.

## Why it matters

- Admins/IT: Enter carrier, call minutes, numbers, retention periods and storage volumes so telephony and Dataverse consumption are represented instead of hidden in a licence-only estimate.
- Leadership/Business: Use the monthly and yearly views to test rollout assumptions and challenge quotes, while treating implementation and run figures as illustrative and validating final numbers with Microsoft or a CSP.
- Makers: Model Copilot Studio answers, actions, tenant-data grounding and AI agent usage with realistic volumes because consumption can exceed licence costs.
