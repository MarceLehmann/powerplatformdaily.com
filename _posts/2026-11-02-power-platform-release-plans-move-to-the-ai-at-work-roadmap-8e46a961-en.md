## TL;DR

According to the source of 2026-10-01, Microsoft planned to stop newly publishing Dynamics 365 and Power Platform Release Plans after September 2026, moving Power Platform, Dynamics 365 and Dataverse roadmap information to AI at Work roadmap. The September 2026 transition has passed; existing plans remain historical references for now.

## Why it matters

- Admins/IT: Update internal procedures and bookmarks from Release Planner or Release Plans to AI at Work roadmap after the September 2026 transition.
- Leadership/Business: Use product filters, CSV export, or RSS when release tracking needs to continue across Power Platform, Dynamics 365, and Dataverse.
