## TL;DR

Copilot Studio’s Customer Conversation harness coordinates customer-facing voice and chat through a primary agent, specialist agents, reusable skills, tools and human escalation. The capabilities are rolling out in public preview. Speech channels do not support end-user sign-in, so knowledge requiring those credentials is limited to chat.

## Why it matters

- Makers: Prototype customer journeys with a primary agent, specialist agents, reusable skills and escalation, while preserving conversation context across handoffs.
- Admins/IT: Track the public preview rollout and channel constraints, especially the lack of end-user sign-in for speech channels and the resulting knowledge limitation.
- Leadership/Business: Identify service scenarios where voice, chat and human handoff can work together, such as order enquiries involving delivery, billing and exceptions.
