## TL;DR

Copilot Studio agents can request human input through Teams or email, collect the response, and use it to steer the agent’s next action when an autonomous decision is uncertain or too risky.

{% include curated-attribution.html %}

## Why it matters

- Makers: Build agents that request human decisions through Teams or email when the agent should not decide alone.
- Leadership/Business: Identify decisions where human review is needed because autonomous agents may encounter unexpected or risky situations.
