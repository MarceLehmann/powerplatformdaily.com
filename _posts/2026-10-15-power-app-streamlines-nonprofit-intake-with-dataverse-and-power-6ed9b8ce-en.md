## TL;DR

Accem’s nonprofit intake solution uses a mobile canvas app, a model-driven back-office app, Dataverse, Power Automate, SharePoint and Power BI. The process reduced registration from more than 10 minutes to two minutes, made documents available in 15 minutes and provided shared access to referrals.

## Why it matters

- Makers: Design intake as connected mobile and back-office experiences, using one Dataverse record and automated validation, approval and incident flows.
- Admins/IT: Apply role-based permissions and Entra ID authentication because the solution handles especially sensitive beneficiary information.
- BI/Data teams: Connect Power BI to the shared data so teams can monitor operations and use impact information for funding justification and decisions.
- Leadership/Business: Measure success by people served and response time, not only by active users or licenses, because the case links digital work directly to service capacity.
