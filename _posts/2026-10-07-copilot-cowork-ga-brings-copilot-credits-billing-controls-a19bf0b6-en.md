## TL;DR

Copilot Cowork is generally available and uses Copilot Credits billed separately from the required Microsoft 365 Copilot subscription. Microsoft 365 admin center Cost Management supports Capacity Packs or pay-as-you-go, monthly and per-user limits, alerts, and security-group scoping for a controlled rollout.

{% include curated-attribution.html %}

## Why it matters

- Admins/IT: Verify billing and policy roles, review existing Copilot Credit allocations, and choose Capacity Packs or pay-as-you-go before activation.
- Admins/IT: Configure monthly and per-user limits, alerts, and a security-group scope so the pilot has measurable boundaries.
- Leadership/Business: Treat the launch as a bounded pilot and expand access only after usage and credit consumption are understood.
