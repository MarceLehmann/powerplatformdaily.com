## TL;DR

Copilot Studio agents in the Frontier rollout can receive their own Microsoft 365 identity and mailbox, share business-process memory across users, and start work from events or schedules. Publishing and activation are required, usage is metered, and agent accounts currently support document generation but not targeted editing or coauthoring of existing documents.

## Why it matters

- Makers: Pilot one small shared process with synthetic information and two users to inspect memory, tool identity and trigger behavior.
- Admins/IT: Confirm the early-release environment, administrator activation and licensing prerequisites before promising an agent-account pilot.
- Leadership/Business: Define an owner, task and reviewable output because shared memory can expose summaries beyond the people who accessed the original content.
