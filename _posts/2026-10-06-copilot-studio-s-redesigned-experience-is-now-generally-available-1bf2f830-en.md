## TL;DR

Copilot Studio’s redesigned experience is now generally available after a short preview period. It introduces Markdown-based SKILL.md instructions, a new visual workflow designer, persistent user context, agent-as-a-node, inline AI, human approval steps and direct Microsoft 365 Copilot integration. The classic and new experiences have no direct interoperability.

## Why it matters

- Makers: Begin learning the new Copilot Studio experience now, because its main capabilities have reached GA after a short preview period.
- Makers: Explore SKILL.md skills, visual workflows, memory, agent-as-a-node, inline AI, human approval, and the Microsoft 365 Copilot node when designing agents.
- Makers: Plan migrations carefully because the classic and new experiences have no direct interoperability, while navigation changes from nine tabs to four.
