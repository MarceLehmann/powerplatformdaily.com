## TL;DR

Copilot Studio’s Conversation harness entered public preview on October 21. It combines a primary customer-facing agent, specialised sub-agents, channel-specific voice and chat instructions, conversation events, native voice testing, and deployment to D365 Contact Center in one building experience.

{% include curated-attribution.html %}

## Why it matters

- Makers: Start with a lightweight primary agent and clearly bounded specialists, then test intent recognition and delegation before adding tools or transactional integrations.
- Admins/IT: Validate channel prerequisites, workstreams, escalation destinations, and D365 Contact Center connectivity before deployment.
- Leadership/Business: Assess whether coordinated specialists can improve customer-facing service experiences across web chat and telephony without requiring customers to choose the right specialist.
