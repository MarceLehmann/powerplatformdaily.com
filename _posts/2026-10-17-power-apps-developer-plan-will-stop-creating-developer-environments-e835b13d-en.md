## TL;DR

From 15 November 2026, Power Apps Developer Plan sign-ups will no longer create developer environments. Existing unmanaged developer environments will be suspended on 15 December 2026 and permanently deleted on 15 January 2027, while managed environments require compatible commercial premium licensing to run apps or flows.

{% include curated-attribution.html %}

## Why it matters

- Makers: Export managed and unmanaged solutions before 15 December 2026, because unmanaged developer environments will then be suspended and later deleted.
- Makers: Use 30-day Trial environments for occasional premium capability testing when no license is assigned.
- Admins/IT: Filter Type = Developer and Managed = No in the Power Platform admin center, then enable Managed Environments or migrate needed solutions to Sandbox when compatible licenses are available.
