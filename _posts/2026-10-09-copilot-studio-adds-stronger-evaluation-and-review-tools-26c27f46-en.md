## TL;DR

Copilot Studio’s September roundup confirms Foundry IQ integration and the Review panel as generally available. Evaluations now cover task completion, tool accuracy, safety and latency, with reusable custom graders. Self-learning and the Skills catalog remain in development with October general availability targets.

{% include curated-attribution.html %}

## Why it matters

- Makers: Use the Copilot Studio Review panel to find agents that were never evaluated or changed models, then rerun checks for task completion, tool accuracy, safety and latency.
- Makers: Give reviewers the Evaluation Viewer role where appropriate so evaluation results can be checked separately from agent building.
- Leadership/Business: Treat self-learning and the Skills catalog as in development, not shipped capabilities, and plan pilots around maker review, testing and publishing.
