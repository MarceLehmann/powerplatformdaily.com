## TL;DR

According to the source of 2026-11-18, agent skills modelled on Anthropic&\#39;s Claude feature have spread across Microsoft Copilot within a year: they run in Microsoft 365 apps, with agents in Agent Builder and Copilot Studio, in SharePoint, OneDrive and Cowork, and Dataverse has its own take. Word (plugin registry MC1384008) and Copilot Chat are still planned, and existing checklists convert to skills with light editing.

## Why it matters

- Makers: Audit existing SOPs and checklists, since the source reports they usually need only light editing to become agent skills usable in Copilot Studio and Agent Builder.
- Leadership/Business: Treat the past year of skills landing across Copilot as the signal to pilot converting documented processes rather than waiting for new tooling.
- Admins/IT: Track the still-planned items, Word support via plugin registry MC1384008 and skills in Copilot Chat, before rolling out user guidance.
