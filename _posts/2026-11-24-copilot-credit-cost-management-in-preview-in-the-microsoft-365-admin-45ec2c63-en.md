## TL;DR

The source of 2026-11-24 reports: Copilot Credit cost management is now in preview in the Microsoft 365 admin center, showing usage, trends, remaining capacity, alerts and spending policies with user-based limits (tenant or group) or agent-based limits (environment), plus billing options like capacity packs, P3 and pay-as-you-go. Makers see credits while building; workflow reporting breaks credit use down per run and node.

## Why it matters

- Admins/IT: Review environment layout before setting agent-based limits, because every agent in an environment shares one limit; start with alerts and name a budget owner for each production agent.
- Makers: Use the credit display next to the test pane and per-node workflow reports to compare design options; treat test credit counts as a lower bound, not a forecast.
- Leadership/Business: Own the budget number for each agent, read the alerts, and stay on pay-as-you-go until trend data supports capacity packs or P3.
