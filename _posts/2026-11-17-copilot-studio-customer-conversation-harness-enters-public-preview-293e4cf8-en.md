## TL;DR

According to the source of 2026-11-17, Copilot Studio has a fourth harness option in public preview: the customer conversation harness. It provides a builder focused on conversational agents for chat and speech, an agent team concept for complex scenarios, Teams handoff for smaller needs, enterprise contact center integrations, Agent 365 visibility, and usage-based billing for building and running.

## Why it matters

- Makers: Try the customer conversation harness in public preview for chat and speech agent builds; the source points to more capabilities in the coming weeks.
- Admins/IT: Budget for usage-based billing on both building and running these agents and use Agent 365 to keep them visible, per the 2026-11-17 source.
- Leadership/Business: Compare the Teams handoff option for smaller support teams against enterprise contact center integration when scoping conversational AI.
