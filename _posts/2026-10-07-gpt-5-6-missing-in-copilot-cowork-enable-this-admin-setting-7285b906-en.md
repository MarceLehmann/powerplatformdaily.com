## TL;DR

GPT 5.6 Sol and GPT 5.6 Terra appear in Copilot Cowork after a Global Administrator or AI Administrator enables OpenAI under Microsoft 365 admin center Copilot settings and chooses access for all users, no users, or specific users and groups.

{% include curated-attribution.html %}

## Why it matters

- Admins/IT: Open Copilot &gt; Settings &gt; View all &gt; AI providers operating as Microsoft subprocessors, expand OpenAI, and start with a security group to validate access in Cowork.
- Admins/IT: Review the processing terms, cloud availability, and July 24, 2026 default change before expanding access, because eligible commercial tenants otherwise enable the models for all users.
