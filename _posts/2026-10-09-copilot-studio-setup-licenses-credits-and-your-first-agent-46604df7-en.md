- question: Which Copilot Studio licenses are needed for a standalone deployment?
  answer: A standalone deployment needs a tenant license for the organisation and
    a user license for each maker. End users do not need licenses.
- question: How do Copilot Credits work in Copilot Studio?
  answer: Copilot Credits measure compute from retrieval, reasoning, actions, tool
    calls and interactions. They can come from pay-as-you-go, capacity packs or pre-purchase
    plans.
- question: How do I create and test a first Copilot Studio agent?
  answer: Create a Power Platform environment, configure licensing and billing, then
    choose Create, New agent and describe its purpose. Test it before publishing.

## TL;DR

Copilot Studio requires maker licensing for builders and Copilot Credits for agent usage. The guide compares trial, Teams and standalone plans, explains pay-as-you-go and capacity options, and outlines five setup steps: licensing, environment provisioning, billing configuration, agent creation and testing. It also shows how agents can invoke Power Automate flows and custom connectors.

## Why it matters

- Makers: Confirm whether trial, Teams or standalone access supports the channels, generative features and connectors your agent needs.
- Admins/IT: Provision a dedicated environment, assign the required tenant and maker access, and link a billing policy before production use.
- Leadership/Business: Measure real credit consumption with pay-as-you-go before committing to a capacity pack, because agent actions and generative responses consume credits.
