## TL;DR

Work IQ business applications enter public preview on September 30, connecting Dynamics 365 and Power Platform data to Copilot and agents. Dataverse semantic models use selected tables, columns, relationships, views, forms and business terminology. Makers can refine this context and reuse business skills across connected agents, including Copilot Studio.

- Makers: Test whether a Dataverse semantic model reduces repeated prompt context across Copilot Studio agents during the public preview.
- Admins/IT: Decide which environments and data can participate before enabling Work IQ business context.
- BI/Data teams: Review the selected Dataverse tables, relationships and business terminology because they define how agents interpret data.
