## TL;DR

According to the source dated 2026-11-20, Business Applications in Work IQ reached general availability for Copilot Chat, Agent Builder and Copilot Studio. Work IQ uses Dataverse app forms, views and relationships to help Copilot identify case ownership and customer links, while existing permissions continue to apply.

## Why it matters

- Makers: Test a trusted model-driven app view with Copilot and compare answers with its records, so the generated context can be checked.
- Admins/IT: Verify access using users with different record visibility, because the source says existing permissions continue to apply.
- BI/Data teams: Use the app&\#39;s forms, views and relationships as context for business questions, while checking source links against the underlying records.
