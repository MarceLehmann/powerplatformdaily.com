## TL;DR

Microsoft will retire Release Wave 1 and Release Wave 2 for Dynamics 365 and Power Platform from September 2026. New capabilities will be shared continuously once plans are committed and ready, while the AI at Work roadmap becomes the central place to track future features and deployment progress.

{% include curated-attribution.html %}

## Why it matters

- Admins/IT: Follow the AI at Work roadmap to track committed capabilities and deployment progress across Power Platform and related Microsoft products.
- Leadership/Business: Replace six-month planning assumptions with continuous roadmap monitoring so adoption plans can respond to capabilities shared throughout the year.
- Admins/IT: Continue monitoring Microsoft documentation for deprecation and removal notices, which will still be published continuously to support timely system updates.
