## TL;DR

Per the source dated 2026-11-26, Teams Phone has three AI automation paths: Intelligent call delegation, a Frontier feature expected to reach GA in January 2027; Teams Phone Agent, GA since September 2026 as a business-wide AI receptionist; and Copilot Studio&\#39;s Customer Conversation harness, in public preview since late October. The first is covered by the Microsoft 365 Copilot license so far; the others are consumption-based.

## Why it matters

- Makers: Try the Customer Conversation harness, in public preview since late October, to deliver voice and chat agents that plug into Teams Phone, chat and contact center scenarios.
- Admins/IT: Compare the three routes before rollout, as Intelligent call delegation is configured per user while the other two options bill by usage volume and call complexity.
- Leadership/Business: Consider delegating call answering to AI; the source reports Teams Phone Agent reached general availability in late September 2026, and delegation licensing may still change.
