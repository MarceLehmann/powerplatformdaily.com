## TL;DR

According to the source of 2026-11-21, Jason Moore told Ignite 2026 that the Copilot Studio version released in August was built entirely by agents. In Microsoft&\#39;s own acceptance tests with the same model, the new Copilot Harness lifted knowledge quality from 66.0 to 86.4 percent and cut input tokens per conversation by 45 percent, while the Evaluate tab and FinOps for Agents reached general availability.

## Why it matters

- Makers: Invest in evaluation suites instead of hand-tuning prompts; build a golden set of 20 to 50 real cases and combine quality, task completion, tool use and safety graders.
- Admins/IT: Watch the Monitor tab, in preview, and use the generally available FinOps for Agents to assign budgets and cascading limits before agent sprawl drives credit spend.
- Leadership/Business: Treat evaluation and governance as the core engineering skill now that agents can build the product, and measure cost per result rather than price per token.
