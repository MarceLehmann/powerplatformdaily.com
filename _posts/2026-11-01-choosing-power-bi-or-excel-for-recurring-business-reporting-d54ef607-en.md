## TL;DR

Excel suits hands-on calculations, scenario work and one-off analysis, while Power BI supports connected data, reusable semantic models and interactive reports. For recurring reporting, the source recommends mapping decisions, audience, refresh needs, ownership and maintenance work before choosing either tool or a hybrid workflow.

## Why it matters

- Makers: Keep Excel for quick checks and scenario analysis, then move stable recurring measures into a maintained Power BI model to avoid rebuilding work.
- BI/Data teams: Test the entire Power BI refresh path, including credentials, gateways and capacity, because scheduled refresh depends on the source and configuration.
- Leadership/Business: Define whether teams need a fixed snapshot or self-service filtering and drill-down, then account for ongoing maintenance and licensing costs.
