## TL;DR

According to the source of 2026-10-31, Microsoft announced Copilot Studio agent accounts, business process memory, and triggers that start work from configured events or schedules. The capabilities are intended for persistent agents that can use approved credentials, retain relevant context across recurring tasks, and participate in ongoing business processes.

## Why it matters

- Makers: Explore agent accounts, process memory and configured triggers when designing recurring Copilot Studio work.
- Leadership/Business: Keep sensitive decisions, exceptions and approvals under human oversight when delegating ongoing processes to agents.
