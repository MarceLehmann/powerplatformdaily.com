## TL;DR

Power Apps model-driven apps require Microsoft Dataverse and use a data-first design. Define tables, columns and relationships first, then configure forms, views, business rules, charts, dashboards and sitemap navigation for users managing connected records and repeatable processes.

## Why it matters

- Makers: Map core records, columns and relationships before configuring forms, views and navigation, so the app reflects real work.
- Makers: Choose model-driven apps for connected, structured records and repeatable processes, and use canvas apps when custom screen design leads the need.
- Leadership/Business: Identify how users enter, find, review and manage records before selecting an app style, reducing poorly organised solutions.
