## TL;DR

Microsoft 365 Copilot Agent Builder suits knowledge questions and document retrieval, Copilot Studio adds connectors, actions and process automation, while Microsoft Foundry targets custom architectures, model control and advanced multi-agent orchestration. The recommended approach is to start with the simplest platform that meets the business need, then add complexity only when required.

## Why it matters

- Makers: Start knowledge-focused experiments in Agent Builder, then use Copilot Studio when an agent must connect to business applications, trigger flows or update records.
- Admins/IT: Define the connectors, actions and sharing boundaries available in Copilot Studio, because the platform supports more active business processes.
- Leadership/Business: Select platforms by business outcome, data access, autonomy and technical control instead of choosing the most powerful option by default. Microsoft reports internal growth from about 2,000 to 20,000 active monthly agent creators, but those results are not guaranteed elsewhere.
