## TL;DR

Model-Driven Apps provide personal views, consistent lookup behavior and URL addressability out of the box. Code Apps offer greater flexibility, but rebuilding these platform capabilities requires additional design, development, testing and maintenance. The better choice depends on whether an application is data-centric and process-oriented or needs a specialized digital product experience.

## Why it matters

- Makers: Check whether personal views, shared filters, lookup behavior and record links are essential before rebuilding a Model-Driven App, because Code Apps may require custom implementations for these capabilities.
- Leadership/Business: Compare the value of greater Code App flexibility with the effort of replacing mature platform functionality, especially for data-centric and process-oriented applications.
- Admins/IT: Watch for consistency and long-term maintenance responsibilities when multiple developers or AI-generated code replace standardized platform behavior.
