Power Platform News brings useful Copilot Studio, Power Apps, Power Automate, Power BI and Microsoft Fabric articles from different blogs into one place. Each curated entry offers a short TL;DR, a practical takeaway and a direct link to the detailed original article.

## Why this exists

I built this for myself as much as for anyone else. There are excellent Power Platform blogs scattered all over the web, and I was tired of checking a dozen feeds by hand to stay current on new features and releases. This site watches them for me and surfaces what's actually worth reading, in one place.

## What we cover

- New features, product updates and releases across Copilot Studio, Power Apps, Power Automate, Power BI and Microsoft Fabric
- Roadmap announcements and what changes for makers and admins
- Integrations and connectors
- Practical how-to guidance

Governance, security, compliance, DLP, ALM, CoE and administration topics are covered by our sister site, [Power Platform Governance](https://powerplatformgovernance.com/), not here.

## Editorial approach

We help you find worthwhile reading, not replace it. Community suggestions are reviewed before publication. We identify the original blog and author, distinguish the source's publication date from our entry date, and link directly to the original. We do not republish full articles or translate them in full.

The original author and the person who suggests a link are not necessarily the same person. Contributor names and LinkedIn profiles appear only with permission. A submitted RSS feed is not blanket permission to republish its contents.

Authors can request a correction or removal by emailing [info@kmupower.com](mailto:info@kmupower.com). Listing a source does not imply a partnership or endorsement.

Product capabilities change, so verify requirements against current Microsoft documentation before applying changes in production.
